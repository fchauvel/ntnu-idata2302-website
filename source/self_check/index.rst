===========
Self-Checks
===========

Self-checks are short, ungraded problem sets that let you see where
you stand at key points in the course. They are particularly useful if
you have chosen not to attend the lectures or the lab sessions. Each
self-check comes with a separate solution sheet, which is released
later so that you get a chance to try the problems first.
